Common questions

Ara won't talk back to me.

Ara needs your own xAI API key. Open Settings › Ara and paste it in. Without a key, tracking, sensors, and export all work normally, but Ara's conversation is turned off. Keys come from x.ai/api and usage is billed to your own account, so you stay in control of the cost.

Why does Pacera ask for background location?

So your run keeps recording accurately with the phone locked in a pocket or on an armband. Without it, iOS suspends the app and your route develops gaps. Pacera never uses location when you aren't tracking an activity.

My heart rate monitor won't connect.

Check that the strap isn't already paired to another app or watch — most BLE sensors only hold one connection at a time. Confirm Bluetooth permission is granted in iOS Settings › Pacera. If you wear an Apple Watch, you can skip the strap entirely and let Pacera read heart rate from Apple Health instead.

How do I get my activities out?

Every activity exports as a standard GPX file you can share anywhere or keep as a backup — open the activity and use the share action. Files also live in Pacera's folder in the Files app. If you've connected Strava in Settings, you can send activities there directly.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual activities and conversation logs inside the app. Deleting Pacera removes everything it stored, since none of it lives on a server. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does Pacera need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no profile on anyone's server. Your data stays on your phone — see the privacy policy for the three narrow exceptions.

I want to report a bug or ask for a feature.

Email the address above. Bug reports with a date, time, and description of the activity are the ones that get fixed fastest.
